# Scientific and product limitations

StateFlow organizes self-observation; it does not measure an objective latent mental state.

- **Observational data:** correlations, before/after differences, and lagged patterns cannot establish cause.
- **Self-report bias:** ratings depend on memory, interpretation, current context, and willingness to record.
- **Missing-not-at-random:** people often record more during unusual periods. Missing days are not assumed average.
- **Context confounding:** “walking” can co-occur with free time, daylight, music, or social contact.
- **Bounded scales:** 0–10 ratings are summarized numerically although adjacent psychological distances may differ.
- **Multiple comparisons:** exploring many pairs produces chance patterns; effect sizes and N are shown rather than proof language.
- **Medication comparisons:** timing, concurrent changes, adherence, adaptation, and regression to the mean remain confounders. The app never recommends changing treatment.
- **Timezone/travel:** preserved IANA zones keep instants correct, but rapid travel still needs interpretation.
- **Synthetic demo:** relationships are planted into noisy generated data for product demonstration only.
- **Safety boundary:** the app does not monitor emergencies, contact others, lock users out, or infer risk from a number.
